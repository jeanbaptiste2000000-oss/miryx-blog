“I’ve been busy” – what hidden doubts linger in a partner’s short text?

You glance at the phone, see the words “I’ve been busy,” and wonder if it’s just a schedule or something deeper. The same phrase can feel like an excuse or a silent alarm. Your mind starts replaying every recent conversation, looking for clues. You want to answer, but you’re not sure what tone to use.

Miryx reading · fictional example

“I've been busy”

Low signal · Miryx benchmark: 20–35%

It can mean the partner truly has a packed agenda and isn’t ignoring you it can mean the partner is avoiding a deeper conversation and uses busyness as a shield.

A clearer version to send: “I’ve had a lot on my plate lately, but I still want to hear from you.”

Three moves for your next text

  1. Ask a specific follow‑up question that invites detail, like “What’s been keeping you busy today?”
  2. Mirror the tone you want to set by keeping your reply calm and open, avoiding accusations
  3. If the reply feels vague, suggest a brief check‑in call to clear any uncertainty
