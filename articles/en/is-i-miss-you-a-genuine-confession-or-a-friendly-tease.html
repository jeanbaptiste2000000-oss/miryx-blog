Is “I miss you” a genuine confession or a friendly tease?

You’ve just opened the chat and see a short, heartfelt line that makes your heart skip. You wonder if it’s a sign they’re ready to move beyond the friendzone or just a momentary feeling. The words feel warm, but the silence before them leaves you uneasy. You start replaying the conversation in your head, searching for clues. The next text could change everything, if you know how to read it.

Miryx reading · fictional example

“I miss you”

Strong signal · Miryx benchmark: 65–80%

It can mean they are thinking about you and want to deepen the connection. It can mean they enjoy the comfort of your friendship and are expressing a platonic longing.

A clearer version to send: “I’ve been thinking about you and miss spending time together”

Three moves for your next text

  1. Ask a clarifying question that invites more detail, like “What made you think of me today?” to gauge emotional depth
  2. Match the tone of the original message; if it’s brief, keep your reply concise but sincere to maintain consistency
  3. If you’re unsure, reply with a light affirmation and a question, such as “I miss you too—what’s been going on?” to open the conversation further

The score measures signal intensity, not a person's worth or the relationship's. A reading is a hypothesis, never a certainty.
