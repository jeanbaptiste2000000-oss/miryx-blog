“We’ll see” – what’s really behind the pause and why the chat went silent

You’ve just sent a hopeful message and the reply lands as a brief “We’ll see.” The conversation stalls, the typing indicator fades, and you’re left wondering if you’ve hit a wall. The silence stretches, and you start replaying every word you wrote. You want to know whether it’s a polite brush‑off or a genuine pause.

Miryx reading · fictional example

“We'll see”

Low signal · Miryx benchmark: 20–35%

It can mean the person is unsure and needs more time before committing to an answer, or it can mean they are gently pulling back without saying a direct no.

A clearer version to send: “I need a little more time to think about it before I give you an answer”

Three moves for your next text

  1. Ask a clarifying question that invites a concrete answer, like “When do you think you’ll know?”
  2. Mirror the tone but add a small prompt, for example reply with “Sounds good, let me know when you’re ready.”
  3. If the silence continues, send a brief follow‑up after a day or two to show you’re still interested without pressuring them
