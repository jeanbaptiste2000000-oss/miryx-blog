That pause breaks with “I miss you” and opens the conversation

You’ve been scrolling through old chats, the last message still glowing on the screen. The silence feels heavier than any argument you ever had. Then a new ping appears, just three words that make your heart skip. You wonder what’s really behind them and how to answer without overthinking.

Miryx reading · fictional example

“I miss you”

Strong signal · Miryx benchmark: 65–80%

It can mean the sender is finally acknowledging the emptiness left by the break and wants to test the waters, it can mean they are looking for reassurance that you still care and are open to a possible reconnection.

A clearer version to send: “I’ve been thinking about you and I miss you”

Three moves for your next text

  1. Ask a clarifying question in your reply to narrow down the sender’s intention, for example “What made you think of me today?”.
  2. Mirror the tone of the original message; if it’s short and simple, keep your response concise too.
  3. Delay your reply just enough to show you’re thoughtful, but not so long that the momentum fades.
