What “I don’t know what to think of us anymore” really says about your situation

You’ve been texting back and forth for weeks, sharing jokes and late‑night thoughts, yet the conversation suddenly stalls. One day a reply arrives that feels both familiar and unsettling. You read, “I don’t know what to think of us anymore,” and wonder if it’s a cry for clarity or a gentle step back. The uncertainty hangs in the air, and you’re left guessing the next move.

Miryx reading · fictional example

“I don't know what to think of us anymore”

Strong signal · Miryx benchmark: 55–75%

It can mean the sender is wrestling with mixed emotions and needs space to sort out their feelings, it can mean they are testing your reaction to gauge whether you’re willing to define the relationship.

A clearer version to send: “I’m not sure how I feel about us right now, can we talk about it?”

Three moves for your next text

  1. When you reply, echo their uncertainty with a calm tone and ask a specific question about their feelings, avoiding assumptions.
  2. Use short, clear sentences to keep the conversation focused on the core issue rather than drifting into unrelated topics.
  3. If you’re unsure, suggest a brief pause to reflect before continuing, and let them know you’re open to talking when both are ready.
