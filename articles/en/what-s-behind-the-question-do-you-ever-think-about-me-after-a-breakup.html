What’s behind the question “Do you ever think about me?” after a breakup

You stare at your phone, the notification lights up, and the words “Do you ever think about me?” appear. Your heart skips a beat, and a flood of memories rushes back. You wonder if it’s a genuine longing or a test of your reaction. The silence that followed your last conversation suddenly feels heavy, and you’re not sure whether to answer or stay quiet.

Miryx reading · fictional example

“Do you ever think about me?”

Strong signal · Miryx benchmark: 55–70%

It can mean the sender is still emotionally attached and hopes to gauge your feelings. It can also be a subtle way to keep the door open without directly asking for reconciliation.

A clearer version to send: “I’ve been thinking about us and wonder if you ever think about me too.”

Three moves for your next text

  1. Take a moment to read the message twice before replying, so you can separate the immediate emotional reaction from the actual words.
  2. If you’re unsure about the tone, reply with a neutral question that invites clarification, like “What made you ask?”
  3. Keep your response concise and honest; avoid adding extra guilt‑inducing details that could complicate the conversation.
