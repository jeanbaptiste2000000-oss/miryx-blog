What “I don’t know… never mind” really says when they stop replying

You’ve sent a message, waited, and then the chat goes silent. The next notification shows a short reply that feels like a shrug, and suddenly the thread stalls. You wonder if it’s a polite exit, a moment of doubt, or something else entirely. The uncertainty can leave you replaying every word in your mind.

Miryx reading · fictional example

“I don't know… never mind”

Moderate signal · Miryx benchmark: 35–55%

It can mean the person feels uncertain and is pulling back without wanting to discuss further. It can also mean they are politely ending the conversation while keeping the door slightly ajar.

A clearer version to send: “I’m not sure what to say right now, let’s pause this.”

Three moves for your next text

  1. Ask a clarifying question that invites a specific answer, like “Do you want to talk about this later?”
  2. Echo their sentiment briefly before offering a gentle nudge, such as “I hear you, and I’m here if you’d like to continue.”
  3. Set a clear next step in your reply, for example “Let’s catch up tomorrow and see how we feel.”
