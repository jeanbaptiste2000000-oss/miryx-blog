“I've been busy” – the quiet distance growing between you two

You’re scrolling through your phone, hoping for a sign that the spark is still there. Instead you see a short reply that feels more like an excuse than an answer. The conversation stalls, and the silence stretches a little longer each day. You start wondering if the distance is just a busy schedule or something more.

Miryx reading · fictional example

“I've been busy”

Low signal · Miryx benchmark: 20–35%

It can mean the person truly has a packed agenda and can’t give more attention right now. It can also be a polite way of creating space while avoiding a deeper conversation about the relationship.

A clearer version to send: “I’ve got a lot on my plate right now, but I still want to stay connected”

Three moves for your next text

  1. Ask a specific follow‑up question to gauge availability, like “When might you have a few minutes to chat?”
  2. Mirror the tone but add a touch of warmth, for example reply with a brief, caring note that acknowledges their busyness
  3. Set a gentle expectation for response time, such as “I understand you’re busy, just let me know when you’re free to talk.”
