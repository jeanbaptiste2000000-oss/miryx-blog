What does « I’ve been busy » really hide when the chat goes silent?

You send a thoughtful message, wait a few minutes, then see a single reply: « I’ve been busy ». The conversation stalls, and you’re left wondering if the silence means disinterest, a genuine overload, or something else. You check your phone again, hoping for a follow‑up, but nothing comes. That lingering doubt can feel oddly unsettling, especially when you care about the connection. Let’s explore what that short answer might be trying to tell you and how you can move forward.

Miryx reading · fictional example

“I've been busy”

Low signal · Miryx benchmark: 20–35%

it can mean the person truly has a packed schedule and can’t devote mental space to the chat yet; it can mean they use a polite excuse to create distance without confronting the issue directly

A clearer version to send: “I’m swamped right now, but I’ll get back to you when I have a moment.”

Three moves for your next text

  1. If you need clarification, reply with a specific question that invites a concrete answer, e.g., “When do you think you’ll have a free moment?”
  2. When you notice a pattern of brief replies, pause before sending another message and give the other person space to re‑engage on their terms
  3. If you decide to follow up, reference something you both enjoy or a shared plan to rekindle the conversation without pressuring them

The score measures signal intensity, not a person's worth or the relationship's. A reading is a hypothesis, never a certainty.
