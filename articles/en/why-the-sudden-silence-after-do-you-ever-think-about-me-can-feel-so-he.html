Why the sudden silence after “Do you ever think about me?” can feel so heavy

You’ve just sent a hopeful text, “Do you ever think about me?” and the chat goes quiet. The notification badge stays empty, the typing indicator never lights up. You start replaying the moment, wondering if you said too much or not enough. That lingering pause can turn a simple curiosity into a knot of uncertainty.

Miryx reading · fictional example

“Do you ever think about me?”

Strong signal · Miryx benchmark: 55–70%

It can mean the other person is unsure how to answer and is taking time to process their feelings. It can mean they are pulling back and prefer to keep distance without confronting the topic directly.

A clearer version to send: “I’m wondering if you think about me sometimes”

Three moves for your next text

  1. Pause before you hit send; give yourself a moment to check if the tone matches what you truly want to convey
  2. When you receive no reply, wait a reasonable amount of time before following up, and keep the next message light and open‑ended
  3. If you decide to answer, acknowledge the question plainly and add a small, genuine detail to keep the conversation flowing

The score measures signal intensity, not a person's worth or the relationship's. A reading is a hypothesis, never a certainty.
