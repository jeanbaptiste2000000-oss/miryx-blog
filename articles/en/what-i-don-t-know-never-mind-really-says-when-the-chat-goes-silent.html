What “I don’t know… never mind” really says when the chat goes silent

You send a message, wait, and the only answer is a short, hesitant phrase. The conversation stalls and you’re left wondering if the interest faded or if something else is happening. You replay the words in your head, trying to catch any clue. It feels like the dialogue has hit an invisible wall, and you’re not sure how to move forward.

Miryx reading · fictional example

« I don't know… never mind »

Moderate signal · Miryx benchmark : 35–55 %

it can mean the person feels uncertain and wants to close the topic without further discussion, it can mean they are distracted or overwhelmed and are using a polite exit to avoid a longer explanation

A clearer version to send: « I’m not sure what to say right now, let’s talk later »

Three moves for your next text

  1. Give them a short, open‑ended question that invites a clear answer without pressure
  2. Offer a specific time to reconnect, showing you respect their space while keeping the conversation alive
  3. Keep your next message brief and focused on one topic to make replying easy

The score measures signal intensity, not a person's worth or the relationship's. A reading is a hypothesis, never a certainty.
