Why a simple “Lol” can signal a sudden silence in your chat

You send a playful message, then the reply is just “Lol”. The next minute the chat goes dead, leaving you wondering if you said something wrong. You check your phone, hoping for another line, but nothing comes. The silence feels oddly heavy, even though the words were light.

Miryx reading · fictional example

“Lol”

Very low signal · Miryx benchmark: 5–15%

It can mean the person is trying to be friendly while actually feeling unsure how to continue, it can mean the person is disengaged and using a filler to close the exchange without a direct goodbye.

A clearer version to send: “I’m not sure what to say right now, but I’ll get back to you later”

Three moves for your next text

  1. Ask a specific follow‑up question that invites a concrete answer, rather than leaving the conversation open ended
  2. Mirror the tone but add a brief acknowledgement of the pause, showing you noticed the gap without pressuring
  3. If the reply stays vague, give the other person a short window to respond before you decide to move on, and then send a clear next step
