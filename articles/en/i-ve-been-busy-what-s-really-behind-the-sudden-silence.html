“I've been busy”—What’s really behind the sudden silence?

You send a message, wait, and the only reply you get is a short “I’ve been busy.” The chat stalls, and you wonder if the conversation is dying or if there’s a real excuse. Your mind starts filling the silence with doubts and scenarios. You want to know whether to push forward or give space, but you’re not sure what that brief answer really signals.

Miryx reading · fictional example

“I've been busy”

Low signal · Miryx benchmark: 20–35%

It can mean they genuinely have a packed schedule and haven’t found time to write back. It can also mean they are using “busy” as a polite way to distance themselves without a direct confrontation.

A clearer version to send: “I’ve been really busy lately, sorry for the delay in replying”

Three moves for your next text

  1. Ask a specific follow‑up question that shows you understand their busyness, like “When you have a moment, could we finish our plan?”
  2. Mirror their tone by keeping your reply brief but warm, acknowledging their situation without pressuring them
  3. Set a gentle deadline for the next exchange, for example suggest a day or time to reconnect, so the conversation has a clear next step

The score measures signal intensity, not a person's worth or the relationship's. A reading is a hypothesis, never a certainty.
